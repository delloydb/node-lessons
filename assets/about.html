Our Story

Where passion for food meets the art of hospitality — Savory is more than a restaurant, it's a gathering place for those who love life's flavors.

A Dream on a Plate

Founded in 2018, Savory began as a humble dream shared by two childhood friends, Marco Rossi and Elena Vazquez. Both trained in some of Europe's finest kitchens, they returned home with a single vision: to create a space where exceptional food and genuine warmth coexist.

What started as a small 20-seat bistro has grown into a beloved culinary landmark — but our philosophy remains unchanged. Every dish is made from scratch, every ingredient is chosen with intention, and every guest is treated like family.

“We don't just serve food — we craft memories. The table is where life happens, and we're honored to be part of your story.”

👨‍🍳 🥗 🍷 ❤️

Meet the Chef

The heart and soul behind every plate — Chef Marco Rossi

👨‍🍳

Marco Rossi

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

With over 20 years of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across Italy, France, and Spain, Marco brings a world of technique and tradition to every dish. His philosophy is simple: “Let the ingredients speak.”

Marco believes that cooking is an act of love. From hand-rolling fresh pasta to sourcing the finest local produce, he oversees every detail with unwavering passion. When he's not in the kitchen, you'll find him exploring local markets or experimenting with new flavor combinations.

— Marco Rossi
